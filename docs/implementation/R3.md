# R3 Chain intake

**Exit gate:** on testnet, a paid submission is ingested exactly once, and
reused, underpaid, wrong-recipient and foreign-locator proofs are all rejected.
**Status on 27 Nov 2026: built, and the testnet run is waiting on two steps.**

- **Done:** the code, 178 tests, and a live check of the stack. The isolated
  stack came up on test/292, and its watcher scanned netuid 292 on testnet.
- **Waiting:** the full run (`scripts/r3_exit_gate.sh`) needs the coldkey
  password typed by its owner, to pay the fee. The safetensors reference
  weights are published, so the reference miner can pass `validate`.

The bad-proof cases already pass against chain-shaped data in
`tests/chain/test_intake.py`. The gate script repeats them against the real
testnet payment.

## Flow

1. **`radiant-sdk submit` checks first.** It needs a matching validate receipt
   under 24 hours old. `/v1/info` must report the network and netuid in
   `miner.env` (test/292). The campaign's fee recipient (seeded from the
   validator's `RADIANT_FEE_RECIPIENT`) is paid only if the miner trusts it: the owner addresses
   pinned in the SDK for that network (the owner coldkey `5GYf…x34a` on
   testnet), plus any the miner adds in `RADIANT_TRUSTED_FEE_RECIPIENTS`. The
   hotkey must be registered.
2. **Upload.** The miner signs `radiant.solution-upload.v1` with the hotkey,
   covering campaign, hotkey, sha256, code_sha256, size, upload id, expiry,
   network and netuid. `POST /v1/uploads/solution` verifies it and checks the
   campaign is open and the zip is under its cap. It returns a presigned PUT
   pinned to the zip's sha256, with `If-None-Match: *`, under
   `radiant/private/campaigns/<campaign>/solutions/<hotkey>/<uuid>.zip`.
3. **Pay.** A coldkey transfer of the campaign fee, in testTAO on testnet,
   after a confirmation prompt. The proof is the transfer's
   `(block, extrinsic index)`.
4. **Commit.** `r1|<campaign>|sha256:<zip>|code:<code_sha256>|<object key>|<block>|<index>`
   via `Commitments.set_commitment`, signed by the hotkey. It is about 311
   bytes, under finney's 384.
5. **Watcher** (every 30 s). For each registered hotkey's latest commitment,
   in chain order:
   - the campaign must exist and be open
   - it must not already be ingested (first seen per campaign and sha256)
   - the object key must name this campaign and this hotkey
   - the fee proof must be present, paid at or before the commitment, never
     used before, and a successful transfer from the hotkey or its coldkey to
     the recipient, at the fee in force at its block
   - the object must exist, be under the cap, and hash to both committed
     hashes

   A passing commitment becomes a `queued` submission with a short numeric id.
   A failing one goes to `intake_rejections` with its reason, and never claims
   the dedupe slot.

## Rejection reasons

| Reason | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `payment_proof_missing` | No `(block, index)`, and the hotkey isn't fee-exempt |
| `payment_after_commitment` | Paid in a later block than the commitment |
| `payment_ref_already_used` | That transfer already backs a submission |
| `payment_not_a_transfer`, `payment_index_out_of_range` | The proof doesn't point at a transfer |
| `payment_recipient_mismatch` | Paid someone other than the fee recipient at that block |
| `payment_below_fee` | Paid less than the fee in force at that block |
| `payment_signer_not_miner` | Neither the hotkey nor its coldkey sent it |
| `payment_extrinsic_failed`, `payment_outcome_unknown` | The transfer failed on chain, or its outcome can't be read |
| `locator_hotkey_mismatch`, `locator_not_ours` | The object key names another hotkey, or isn't in this validator's bucket |
| `object_missing_or_too_large`, `sha256_mismatch`, `code_sha256_mismatch` | The uploaded bytes aren't what was committed |
| `unknown_campaign`, `campaign_not_open` | As named |

`GET /v1/submissions/{sha256}` and `radiant-sdk status <sha256>` show a
submission's state and events, or why it was rejected.

## Choices to review

1. **Uploads go to Hippius.** The storage probe passed on bucket
   `radiant-subnet`: checksum and If-None-Match are both enforced. MinIO stays
   available as the fallback, switched with a `.env` change or
   `--storage minio` on the gate.
2. **Fee-exempt hotkeys** (`RADIANT_FEE_EXEMPT_HOTKEYS`) skip the fee, as in
   Pareton. That's for the owner's seed leader.
3. **The fee recipient is the owner wallet in the validator's `.env`**
   (`RADIANT_FEE_RECIPIENT`). Seeding writes it into each campaign's fee
   history, outside the manifest hash, and the watcher verifies payments
   against that history. A manifest file may still name its own recipient,
   but the fixture doesn't. The SDK keeps a
   per-network allowlist as a guard, so a wrong or compromised API URL can't
   redirect a payment. It holds the owner coldkey `5GYf…x34a`
   (`rumayao_coldkey`) on testnet, and miners can extend it in `miner.env`.
   Mainnet's list is empty until launch.
4. **Commitments are rate-limited per hotkey by the chain.** So the gate
   checks the reused-payment and foreign-locator cases by replaying them
   through the watcher with the real payment, instead of making more
   on-chain commitments.

## Running it

```bash
scripts/r3_exit_gate.sh --stack-only        # stack + live watcher scan, no payment
scripts/r3_exit_gate.sh                     # full gate: validate, pay 0.01 testTAO, commit, verify
```
